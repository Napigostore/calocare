**Short description (≤80)**
Snap a meal, get the calories. Tap-to-log foods and a personal nutrition coach.

**Full description**

CaloCare shows how much you eat, how much you burn and what to eat next — without typing every meal.

● Log a meal in one tap
Over 240 everyday foods and drinks: eggs, oatmeal, salads, burgers, pizza, pasta, sushi, ramen, curries, lattes, smoothies… plus Vietnamese favourites like phở and bánh mì. Tap once to add a serving, create your own foods, and the app suggests the foods you eat most.

● Snap your meal
Fit the plate inside the green circle and the AI identifies each item, estimating calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar and sodium. Adjust the size (small, medium, large) or the amount before saving, or rebuild a dish from its ingredients in grams.

● A coach that knows you
Ask what to eat tonight, get a menu for tomorrow or a review of your week. Suggestions follow your calories left and your goal: lose weight, maintain, gain, build muscle or run.

● Health mode
Tell the app about diabetes, high blood pressure, gout, high cholesterol, food allergies or a vegetarian, vegan, keto or halal diet. It warns you about foods that don't suit you and leaves them out of suggested menus.

● Connect your watch
Reads active calories, steps, sleep, weight and height through Health Connect (Wear OS, Samsung Health, Fitbit, Garmin, Mi Fitness and more).

● Clear progress
Charts for calories in and out, weight, macros and steps over 7 and 30 days. An adaptive target suggests adjustments based on your real weight trend.

● Private
No account needed. Your data stays on your phone. No ads.

Note: CaloCare provides estimates and general suggestions. It is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or your doctor's advice.
