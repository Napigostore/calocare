CaloCare – Privacy Policy

Tiếng Việt

Updated: 16 October 2026

Summary

CaloCare keeps your profile and log on your phone. The app needs no account, shows no ads and sells no data. Data leaves the device only when you choose to use an AI feature or search for food online, as described below.

Data the app stores on your phone

• Profile: name, sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level, goal.

• Health details you enter: conditions to watch, allergies, diet.

• Food log, workouts, weight history and foods you create.

This data sits in the app's private storage and is not backed up anywhere else.

Data from watches and fitness bands (Health Connect, Apple Health)

When you connect and grant permission, CaloCare only READS: active calories, total calories burned, steps, sleep, weight and height. This data is used only to work out calories burned, draw progress charts and give advice inside the app. CaloCare never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health, never sends this data off the device and never uses it for advertising. You can withdraw permission at any time in Health Connect (Android) or Settings → Health (iPhone).

AI features

When you use an AI feature, the following is sent through CaloCare's server to the AI provider Anthropic for processing:

• Meal photos: the photo and a profile summary (sex, age, height, weight, goal, conditions, allergies, diet).

• Coach chat: the conversation, a profile summary and your log for the last 7 days.

• Food estimates: the food name you typed.

CaloCare's server only forwards these requests and does not store your photos, conversations or profile. To count usage, the server keeps an anonymous device code (a hash containing no name, phone number or email) with the number of requests used.

When you correct a dish the AI got wrong, the two names (the AI's and the one you chose) are sent to CaloCare's server, without the photo or your profile, so the AI recognises that dish correctly for everyone next time.

When you create a new food, or adjust one by its ingredients, its name and nutrition figures (without photos, your profile or your log) are sent to CaloCare's server as a suggestion for the shared food library. A food reaches other users only after the CaloCare team has reviewed it.

If you enter your own API key (Anthropic or DeepSeek) under Advanced, data goes straight to that provider and is handled under its policy.

Searching for food online

When you tap “Find product”, your search words are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), with no personal information.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera (Camera permission) or your photo library only when you choose to photograph a meal. Only the part of the picture inside the guide circle is sent for analysis; the photo is not saved to your log or gallery.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile → “Delete all data” to remove your profile, log and API key from the device. Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data.

Children

CaloCare is not meant for children under 13. Its calorie targets are calculated for adults.

Not medical advice

CaloCare provides estimates and general suggestions about nutrition and exercise. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it does not replace your doctor's advice.
