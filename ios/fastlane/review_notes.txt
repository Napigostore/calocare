PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
CaloGold is a calorie and nutrition tracker for adults who want to lose, maintain or gain weight, build muscle or train for running. Mainstream trackers rarely know Vietnamese dishes and make logging tedious. CaloCare has a built-in table of 240+ Vietnamese and international foods logged with one tap, estimates a meal's calories from a photo, gives health warnings for declared conditions and allergies (diabetes, high blood pressure, gout, high cholesterol, food allergies, vegetarian/keto/halal diets), and offers an AI nutrition coach. It gives general wellness estimates only; it is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anything.

HOW TO USE (no account, no login, no sample files needed)
1. First launch: fill in the short profile (name optional, sex, birth year, height, weight, activity, goal) and tap "Get started".
2. Today tab: calories eaten vs target and burned, the day's meals with photos, the mascot's encouragement and a 7-day chart. Tap a meal tile or "Add a meal" to log food.
3. Log a meal: tap any food to add a serving to the selected meal; search by name; the camera button photographs a meal and the AI lists the dishes with calories, which can be adjusted before adding them.
4. Coach tab: ask nutrition or training questions; answers use the profile and the last 7 days of logs. Progress tab: weight, calories, macros, steps, workouts and sleep over 7 or 30 days.
5. Me tab: suggested menu, meal logging, profile and goals ("Profile and goals"), and photo and data credits. In the profile, "Connect Apple Health" is optional and read-only (active energy, steps, exercise minutes, sleep, weight, height; the data stays on the device).
6. CaloCare Pro (In-App Purchases): in the same profile sheet, tap "Upgrade to Pro". The paywall lists CaloCare Pro Monthly (com.napigo.calocare.pro.Monthly, $1.99 per month) and CaloCare Pro Yearly (com.napigo.calocare.pro.yearly, $17.99 per year, 7-day free trial). "Restore purchase" is on the same screen. The paywall also opens automatically when the free allowance of AI photos or questions runs out. Purchases are not restricted by storefront or device.
Free use includes the first 30 AI photos and 50 coach questions, then 2 photos and 5 questions a day. CaloCare Pro (auto-renewable subscriptions com.napigo.calocare.pro.Monthly and com.napigo.calocare.pro.yearly, yearly with a 7-day free trial) raises this to 40 photos and 100 questions a day. Manual logging, menus, charts and Apple Health are always free.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- CaloCare server (Cloudflare Workers, our own): forwards AI requests, counts the daily allowance per anonymous device hash, serves the shared food library, and verifies App Store transactions with Apple's signature. It stores no photos, chats or profiles.
- Anthropic Claude API (AI provider): recognises foods in meal photos, estimates foods described in text, and answers coach questions. Reached only through our server.
- Apple StoreKit (subscriptions) and Apple HealthKit (optional, on-device).
No accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs.

REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. Its interface is in English or Vietnamese following the device language; English-speaking users see international foods first, Vietnamese users see Vietnamese dishes first.

REGULATION AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry and is not a medical device. It contains no protected third-party material; the app's illustrations and mascot are original artwork by the developer, and dish photos are credited inside the app; nutrition values are our own estimates based on public nutrition tables. Photos analysed are taken by the user.
